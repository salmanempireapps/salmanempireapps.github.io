Frequently Asked Questions

Is Voice Reply: Hands-Free Chat safe to use?

Yes. Voice Reply processes all notifications locally on your Android device and never uploads, stores, or sells your personal chats or contacts on any developer server.

Does Voice Reply read banking SMS, OTPs, or other apps?

No. Voice Reply uses a strict package allowlist limited solely to WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business notifications when explicitly enabled by the user.

Which languages does Voice Reply support?

Voice Reply supports English, pure Hindi, and Roman Hindi (Hindi written in English letters) with natural voice prompts and hands-free dictation.

Does Voice Reply work when the phone screen is off?

Yes. When turned ON by the user, Voice Reply runs a visible foreground notification service that announces incoming WhatsApp messages and sends voice replies even while the screen is locked.
